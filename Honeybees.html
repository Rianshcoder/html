About Honeybees

All About Honeybees

A honey bee is a social, flying insect of the genus Apis known for making wax hives and producing honey. They play a vital role in nature as key pollinators for flowers, fruits, and vegetables.

There are 3 types of bees-
    Queen: One dominant female per hive whose sole job is to lay thousands of eggs to grow the family.
    Workers: Female bees that forage for nectar and pollen, build and clean the honeycomb, feed the young, and protect the hive.
    Drones: Male bees whose main purpose is to mate with a new queen.

Why are they important

Honeybees are vital because they pollinate roughly one-third of the human diet and support global ecosystems. They help grow essential crops like fruits, vegetables, and nuts, while producing useful natural items such as honey and beeswax.
