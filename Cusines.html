Cuisines of India

Lets learn about Cuisines of India

Indian cuisine is a vast collection of regional cooking styles shaped by local history, geography, and culture.

North Indian Cuisines

    Features rich, creamy gravies and dairy products like paneer, ghee, and yogurt.
    Uses wheat-based flatbreads like roti, naan, and paratha.
    Iconic dishes include butter chicken, dal makhani, and kebabs.

South Indian Cuisines

    Centers around rice and rice-fermented dishes like idli and dosa.
    Relies on coconut, tamarind, and local spices with seafood along the coasts.
    Iconic dishes include sambar, rasam, and Hyderabad biryani.

West Indian Cuisines

    Blends coastal seafood speacialties in Goa and Mahrashtra with vegatarian staples in Gujrat.
    Frequently serves balanced thali meals combining sweet, salty, and spicy elements.
      Iconic dishes include Goan vindaloo, misal pav, and dhokla.
