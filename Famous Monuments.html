Famous Monuments

There are many famous monuments in the world

    Taj Mahal: The Taj Mahal is a globally renowned ivory-white marble mausoleum located on the southern bank of the Yamuna River in Agra, India. Commissioned in 1631 by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan to house the tomb of his favorite wife, Mumtaz Mahal, it is recognized worldwide as the pinnacle of Mughal architecture and an enduring symbol of love.
    Statue of Liberty: The Statue of Liberty is a world-famous symbol of freedom and democracy located on Liberty Island in New York Harbor. A gift from the people of France to the United States, this iconic copper monument was dedicated on October 28, 1886, and welcomes millions of visitors each year.
    Eiffel Tower: The Eiffel Tower (La Tour Eiffel) is the world's most famous paid monument and the definitive symbol of Paris, France. Designed by engineer Gustave Eiffel for the 1889 World's Fair, this 330-meter-tall wrought-iron lattice masterpiece welcomes approximately 7 million visitors annually.
    Pyramids of Giza: The Pyramids of Giza are 4,500-year-old royal tombs located on the Giza Plateau on the western outskirts of Cairo, Egypt. Built during the Fourth Dynasty of the Old Kingdom, this UNESCO World Heritage Site includes the Great Pyramid of Giza (Khufu), the Pyramid of Khafre, the Pyramid of Menkaure, and the iconic Great Sphinx. As the oldest and only largely intact structure of the original Seven Wonders of the Ancient World, the complex stands as a global symbol of ancient architectural brilliance.
