All About Pizza

Pizza is a flatbread topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and various items, baked in an oven. It started in Naples, Italy, in the 1700s as a cheap meal for workers. Today, it is one of the most popular foods in the world.

These are the key parts of Pizza.

    Crust: Made from flour, water, yeast, and salt. It can be thin or thick.
      Sauce: Usually made from crushed tomatoes, garlic, and oil. Some use white cream sauces.
      Cheese: Mozzarella is the most common choice.
      Toppings: Popular options include meat, vegetables, and herbs.

    These are the popular types of Pizza.

      Margherita: Tomato, fresh mozzarella, and basil. Colors match the Italian flag.
        Neapolitan: Traditional thin crust cooked fast in a very hot wood oven.
        New York Style: Large, thin slices that you can fold to eat.
        Chicago Deep Dish: Baked in a deep pan with thick crust and cheese and meat on the bottom.
